About Our System

Solar-powered water purification designed for remote and off-grid communities — clean, safe water powered by the sun, monitored in real-time.

Our system harnesses renewable solar energy to purify contaminated or brackish water without reliance on external power grids. Engineered for low-cost, sustainable deployment, it combines advanced filtration with smart IoT monitoring to deliver clean drinking water anywhere.

☀️ Off-Grid

Operates entirely on solar power, no grid connection required. Designed for remote areas with reliable 24/7 clean water output.

📦 Low Cost

Low operational cost using renewable energy and durable filtration membranes.

🖥️ IoT Monitoring

Real-time monitoring via IoT sensors. Track pH, turbidity, flow, and filter status.

Clean Water Using Solar Energy - Key Features
