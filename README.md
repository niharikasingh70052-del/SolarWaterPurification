The Solar Water Purification System is an innovative, eco-friendly, and sustainable project designed to purify water using solar energy. It utilizes sunlight as a natural source of energy to support the water purification process, reducing dependence on conventional electricity and promoting the use of renewable energy.

The main objective of this project is to provide clean and safe water, especially in rural areas, remote villages, and locations where electricity and access to clean drinking water are limited. The system uses solar energy to heat water and help separate it from impurities through an appropriate purification method, such as solar distillation.

This project aims to reduce environmental pollution, conserve energy, and make water purification more affordable and accessible. It also promotes awareness about renewable energy and the importance of sustainable water management.

Conclusion: The Solar Water Purification System is a practical and environmentally friendly solution that combines solar energy with water purification technology to support a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable future.
